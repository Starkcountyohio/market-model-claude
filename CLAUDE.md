## What this project is

QC and review tooling for the **2026 cycle** of Stark County Auditor's market valuation model. The market model is the **value-defense** tool (used at Boards of Revision); the cost model is what taxes are billed on. So "good" here means defensible per-parcel, not just accurate in aggregate.

## 2026 cycle parameters

- **Lien date:** 2025-01-01
- **Sale window:** Jan 2021 → Dec 2025 (60 months — 4 years before lien date, ~12 months after)
- **Cycle phase:** Interim / off-cycle update (not sexennial reappraisal, not triennial update)
- **Sales updated:** dropped 2020 sales, added 2025 sales; duplicates removed

- **SFR (Land Use 510):** Clusters 1–6, Models 100–600. 52 NGroups, RS-prefixed neighborhoods. SFR cluster assignments in `Model-Cluster-NGroup-NBHD Assignments.xlsx`.

The "3-segment spline" labeled in `Time adjustment for 2025 version.xlsx` Sheet6 is an analytical sketch, NOT what's wired into the 2026 model. SFR clusters use a single linear time term; only condo has a one-knot spline (SPLINE0 = newest 22 months).

The other two spline-segment variables (SPLINE15, SPLINE42) are defined in MKVAR but not used by any model.

**Finding 3 — All cluster linear coefficients match empirical within 10%, and per-cluster spline uplift is modest (≤ 0.04 R²).**
The single linear term per SFR cluster is empirically defensible. See sheets `7_Cluster_Slope_QC` and `8_Per_Cluster_Spline_Fit` in `Time_Adjustment_QC_2026.xlsx`.

Cluster segment slopes go in different directions — clusters 1, 4, 7 mirror the county-wide "flat middle" pattern, but clusters 2 and 6 show the opposite (steepest in middle). Confirms cluster-level fitting is the right unit.

## Conventions

- **month_idx is FORWARD-time:** 0 = oldest month, 59 = newest. iasWorld's `Months Number` is BACKWARD (0 = newest); we flip on read.
- **MKMODVAR SALMNXSFLA coefficient** ≈ forward-time PPSF slope in $/sqft/month. iasWorld's internal sign handling makes the positive backward-variable coefficient produce forward-positive predictions; verified empirically by binning MRAVAL/SFLA by month_idx.
- **NBHD prefixes:** `RS*` = SFR, `RC*` = condo.
- **Cluster → model number:** cluster k → model k × 100 (1→100, …, 7→700).
